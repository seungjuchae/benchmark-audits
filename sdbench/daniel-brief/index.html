What I would not present as an established defect

  • Generating absent results with is_synthetic=true is explicitly permitted by the current Gatekeeper prompt; clinical incorrectness was not established.
  • Astra refusing 10 bundled requests then accepting 31 split requests does not prove inconsistent enforcement; procedure/panel scope and batch context changed together.
  • Bartonella IgG/IgM details exceed the source's explicit wording, but a clinically false value or definite provenance violation was not established.
  • Both natural diagnoses omitted HLH and received 4, consistent with the rubric's incomplete-diagnosis category. These are observed model omissions, not evidence that the grader failed.
  • The unsupported hypogammaglobulinemia addition was rejected (3/5) by both judges in all three repeats each; the proposed false-positive did not reproduce.
  • is_synthetic=false is a model-supplied label and is also used on refusal responses; it is not independent factual verification.
